CrossDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to CrossDrop for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

The short version: CrossDrop collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs. Everything happens on your devices and your local network.

What CrossDrop does on your device

  • Peer discovery — advertises the device name you chose and a random token (new on every launch) on your local Wi-Fi using Bonjour/mDNS so nearby devices can find each other. The token identifies nothing about you. (The Android app can also use Bluetooth Low Energy.)
  • File transfer — sends the files you choose directly to the device you tap, over a direct local connection. Files never pass through a server and never leave your local network. Every transfer is end-to-end encrypted (X25519 key exchange, HKDF-SHA256, ChaCha20-Poly1305) and can be verified with a 6-digit code shown on both devices.
  • Send to anyone / Wireless Drive — when you start them, the app serves the chosen files or folder from a small web server on your local network, protected by a random link and, for the drive, an optional passcode. Browser access uses plain HTTP on your own network; stop sharing at any time from the app. Nothing is reachable from the internet.
  • On-device storage — received files, a local transfer log and your chosen display name are stored only on your device. Delete them any time from Inbox and Settings.

What CrossDrop does not do

  • No data collection, sharing or sale — nothing leaves your device or your network.
  • No tracking, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No account, sign-in, e-mail or personal identifiers.
  • No internet connection is used or needed.

Children

CrossDrop does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
