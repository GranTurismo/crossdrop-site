CrossDrop

Ad-free, privacy-first file sharing between nearby devices — on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. Files travel directly over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

  • Radar — nearby devices appear automatically; tap one to send.
  • Fast & resumable — only changed chunks are re-sent; interrupted transfers pick up where they stopped.
  • Send to anyone — a QR code lets someone with no app download in their browser.
  • Wireless Drive — turn a device into a drive you can open from Finder, Windows Explorer or any browser.
  • Private by design — X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption, a 6-digit code to verify both sides.
